GuitarHUD

Mac app · macOS 13+

A floating chord window that follows whatever Spotify is playing. Play a song and the Ultimate Guitar chords are already up, sitting on top of your other windows.

  • Best version preloaded, with a list of alternatives one click away
  • Hover a chord to see the fingering
  • Transpose, and auto-scroll in time with the song
Download for Mac Source
Needs the Spotify desktop app. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so after dragging it into Applications, run this once in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/GuitarHUD.app

Drafter

Web · runs on claude.ai

Write with Claude one stretch at a time. When a word or idea starts to feel wrong, highlight it. Everything from there gets rewritten, and you keep going until the whole reply is something you agree with.

The quiet is not empty, it is a low-entropy state full of things waiting
  • Tap a reason like wrong energy or wrong concept, or write a note
  • Flagged words stay off-limits for the rest of the reply
  • Every rejected version is kept, so you can bring one back
Open Drafter Source
Sign in with a claude.ai account. Drafter writes with your own Claude plan, and asks before its first use.
